# SmartCare v0.1 - Initial Engineering Brief and AI

1. Problem Summary (Approximately 100 words)

SmartCare Community Clinic currently manages patient information and appointments using a combination of paper records and spreadsheets. This manual process is likely to be time-consuming, inefficient, and susceptible to errors such as misplaced records, duplicate information, and scheduling conflicts. The clinic has requested software that can manage patients, practitioners, and appointments, but this statement represents only an initial description of the problem rather than a full set of requirements. Before development begins, further discussions with the client will be needed to clarify the system's functionality, user roles, security expectations, reporting needs, and any legal or privacy obligations that the software must satisfy.
